**Two timestamp limits remain.** The required `±HH:MM` format cannot encode historical offsets such as Paris’s `+00:09:21`. Calculations use the exact offset, and output displays `+00:09`, so round-tripping that timestamp shifts the instant by 21 seconds. A whole occurrence ending in year 10000 also cannot fit the required format. `occurrences` raises `PlanError` in that case, and `agenda` can still return a clipped segment if its endpoints fit.
