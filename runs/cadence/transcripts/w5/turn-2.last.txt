I audited all 11 sections of `SPEC.md` and found two gaps the passing QA suite missed. Both are fixed.

- A counted monthly rule that could never produce a date still scanned toward distant windows. A 200-rule reproduction dropped from about 1.3 seconds to 3 milliseconds.
- The CLI accepted certain malformed UTF-8 bytes as JSON. It now rejects them with exit status 1 and still accepts valid UTF-8, UTF-16, and UTF-32 plans.

All 29 repository tests passed on Python 3.12, 3.14, and 3.15. The monthly fix also matched direct calendar enumeration across 8,232 cases.

Two limits remain in the timestamp format. Historical UTC offsets can include seconds, which `±HH:MM` cannot represent exactly. I reproduced an apparent 21-second overlap when parsing two adjacent agenda segments from Paris and UTC in 1900. Also, `occurrences` raises `PlanError` when a whole occurrence ends beyond year 9999, while `agenda` can return a clipped segment that fits within the four-digit calendar.
