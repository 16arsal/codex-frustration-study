Implemented both additions in the Python API and CLI, and updated [SPEC.md](~/Projects/ledger-lib/w4/SPEC.md).

- `shares` splits accept positive integer weights and use the same remainder allocation and alphabetical tie-breaking as percentage splits.
- Foreign expenses convert to group-currency cents before splitting, using exact arithmetic and half-even rounding. At the supplied rate, `10.00` EUR becomes `10.82` USD. Exact splits require the group currency.

Supplied rate tables are validated even when unused, and currency keys must be unique after ignoring case.

All 23 tests pass. An independent calculation also matched balances and settlements for 1,000 generated groups.
