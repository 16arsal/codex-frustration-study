Implemented holidays and elapsed minute and hour rules in the Python API and CLI. Updated [SPEC.md](~/Projects/cadence-app/w5/SPEC.md).

- Holidays apply before `times` and overrides. Shifted occurrences keep their original keys, and collisions are resolved at the destination after applying DST rules.
- Minute and hour rules advance by elapsed time, use UTC minute keys, and check holidays and `until` against the local date. Historical starts with nonzero UTC seconds use the containing minute as the key and retain exact seconds in calculations.

All 45 tests passed on Python 3.12, 3.14, and 3.15. A separate reference implementation matched 700 generated plans. The local 200-rule minutely benchmark returned 288,000 occurrences in 0.37 seconds.
